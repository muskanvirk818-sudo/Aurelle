## Brand & Style

This design system channels the restrained poise of high-fashion ateliers and timeless editorial publishing. Inspired by the understated confidence of houses like The Row, Céline, and Totême, it rejects loud, synthetic gimmicks in favor of deliberate space, disciplined alignment, and tactile warmth. 

The aesthetic marries classical editorial serif weight with pristine, functional modernism. The interface acts as an architectural gallery frame: unobtrusive, impeccably proportioned, and quiet enough to let bespoke textiles, cut, and silhouettes command attention. The emotional response is one of calm discernment, uncompromising craftsmanship, and permanence.

## Colors

The palette relies on stark tonal contrast balanced by warm, parchment-like undertones.

- **Primary (`#0A1F44`)**: Midnight Navy serves as the core anchor. It replaces pure black to introduce rich, deep optical grounding across prominent headers, brand marks, and high-emphasis structural blocks.
- **Secondary / Accent (`#C9A86A`, `#D4B87C`, `#9E7D3B`)**: Muted champagne and spun gold. Applied sparingly to critical interactive highlights, collection markers, and delicate active strokes. It never dominates; it gilds.
- **Surfaces & Backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`, `#FBF9F5`, `#F4F1EA`)**: Crisp white is reserved for product isolation stages, while an ivory wash (`#FBF9F5`) and warm stone (`#F4F1EA`) dress canvas sheets and architectural section blocks.
- **Text & Hierarchy**: Primary body copy sits in Deep Slate (`#1C2B46`), supporting body in Charcoal (`#4A5568`), and metadata or secondary attributes in Muted Stone (`#718096`).
- **Dividers & Structural Hairlines**: Subtle gold hairline (`#E6D7B8`) for editorial dividers and polished slate (`#E2E8F0`) for interface utility grids.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional juxtaposition between high-contrast Roman serifs and rational sans-serif legibility.

- **Editorial Serifs**: Playfair Display delivers high-contrast strokes and elegant proportioning. Display titles must maintain generous vertical breathing room; never condense or crowd them.
- **Functional Body**: Inter provides precise clarity for descriptions, dimensions, care labels, and transactional text. Its line-height is intentionally expanded across long-form descriptions to evoke print catalog pacing.
- **Labels & Micro-copy**: Set in small-caps with tracked spacing (`letter-spacing: 0.14em`) for category banners, collection tags, badge indicators, and table headers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a 12-column responsive fluid grid that prioritizes breathing space and editorial composition.

- **Grid Disciplines**: Desktop screens employ a maximum canvas constraint of 1440px with generous margins (`4rem`) and fluid gutters (`1.5rem`). Asymmetrical offsets (e.g., 5-column imagery against 7-column content) are encouraged for lookbook layouts.
- **Mobile Adaptations**: On mobile displays under 768px, margins compress to `1.25rem` with `0.75rem` gutters across a 4-column system. Full-bleed edge treatments are reserved for single-product hero showcases.
- **Whitespace Ratio**: Structural sections must be separated by `space-xl` or dynamic view heights (`8vh` to `12vh`) to uphold the gallery sensibility. Never crowd adjacent product clusters.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews heavy drop shadows in favor of tonal gradation and crisp, paper-thin structural lines.

- **Tonal Layering**: Depth is created by placing crisp white panels over the ivory canvas (`#FBF9F5`), subtly framing product viewports without elevation artifacts.
- **Hairline Outlines**: Boundaries, cards, and modal sheets are delineated using 1px solid borders in soft champagne gold (`#E6D7B8`) or muted slate (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Focus (Overlays & Flyouts)**: Drawers, cart trays, and modal viewports use a backdrop tint of `#0A1F44` with a 35% opacity and a subtle `4px` blur. When depth is required for floating navigation, use an ultra-diffused shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(10, 31, 68, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs razor-sharp tailoring (`0px` border radius). 

- **Architectural Edge**: All structural components—cards, action triggers, text inputs, drawers, and modal views—possess sharp right angles. This echoes the lines of high-end packaging boxes and stationery.
- **Swatch Exceptions**: Circular forms are strictly isolated to functional colorway swatches and round gemstone/material preview beads.

## Components

### Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary**: Sharp rectangular box filled with deep navy (`#0A1F44`) with white typography, or soft gold (`#C9A86A`) with navy text for pivotal conversions (e.g., "Add to Bag"). High padding (`16px 32px`), tracked uppercase text (`label-caps`). On hover: background deepens (`#9E7D3B` or `#06132B`) with a smooth 200ms transition.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: Transparent fill with a 1px hairline border in `#0A1F44` or `#C9A86A`. Text matches the border color.
- **Tertiary (Text-Only)**: Understated uppercase link underlined by a 1px gold hairline spaced 4px beneath the text baseline.

### Product Cards
- Clean vertical frame. Image ratio is set strictly to 3:4 (editorial portrait). 
- Zero drop shadows; bounded by either an optional 1px slate divider or pure open space.
- Hover reveals an alternate garment angle or a subtle inline drawer for direct size addition.
- Metadata below the frame: Category in `label-caps` (`#718096`), Title in `Playfair Display` (`body-md`), Price in clean tabular `Inter` (`body-sm`).

### Form Inputs & Text Fields
- Minimalist underline or border treatment: a single bottom border or crisp 1px enclosure using `#E2E8F0`. 
- Focus state shifts the border to spun gold (`#C9A86A`) without default browser rings.
- Placeholder text in `#718096`, floating smoothly upward into `label-sm` upon entry.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkboxes: Precise square (16px) with a 1px border. Checked state renders a solid `#0A1F44` interior with an ivory micro check mark.
- Radios / Size Buttons: Replaced with typographic size boxes (e.g., "XS", "S", "M", "L"). Unselected sizes feature a hairline slate border; selected states become solid navy with white type. Out-of-stock sizes display a subtle diagonal hairline stroke.

### Swatches & Filter Chips
- **Color Swatches**: Pure circles (20px diameter) bounded by an outer ring that appears with 2px of white breathing space when active.
- **Filter Chips**: Sharp rectangles with 1px hairline slate borders. Active filters feature an accent badge with an ivory ground and gold outline.

### Navigation Trays & Drawers
- The slide-out mini-bag and collection filter drawer extend from the right edge, framed in ivory (`#FBF9F5`) with a 1px champagne line on the interior divider.
- Cart items are structured as distinct horizontal rows separated by hairline rules.
